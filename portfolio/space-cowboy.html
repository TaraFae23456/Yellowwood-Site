Space Cowboy.

A cabin and a geodesic dome in the Hochatown pines, designed as Western meets outer space: saddles and starlight, rusted tin and neon. Sleeps twelve.

The brief.

The guest
Groups of up to twelve, coming for a weekend they’ll talk about long after checkout.
The idea
Western, but make it outer space. Every room takes one side of that line, or sits right on it.
The signature moment
A geodesic dome bedroom: a four-poster under a hand-painted night sky, brass sconces and a telescope aimed through the windows.
The palette
Sage, coral, lilac and mustard against rough cedar, rusted tin and black steel.
The brand
A wordmark, a neon line (“Saddle up among the stars”), a painted dome door, and branded details right down to the cornhole boards.

Room by room.

Photographed this fall. Every room had its own brief, and none of them were beige.
