Airbnb & short-term rental setup: frequently asked questions

What do you need to furnish an Airbnb or short-term rental?

A guest-ready short-term rental needs living-room and bedroom furniture, two full linen sets per bed, bathroom towels and amenities, a fully equipped kitchen, a coffee and beverage station, outdoor seating, guest-experience extras like games and books, plus safety gear, cleaning supplies, and host consumables. This Airbnb furnishing checklist organizes all of it into 16 categories and 295 host-tested products.

How much does it cost to furnish an Airbnb?

For a typical 3-bedroom, 2-bathroom rental sleeping 8, budget roughly $8,000 for essentials only and around $20,000 or more for a full, elevated setup. The cost to furnish a short-term rental scales with bedrooms, bathrooms, and guest count, and this checklist calculates a live estimate as you set your property profile.

What do I need to buy first to set up a short-term rental?

Start with the Essential tier: beds and two linen sets per bed, bath towels, a stocked kitchen, seating, safety items (smoke and CO detectors, fire extinguisher, first-aid), and cleaning supplies. Essentials for a 3-bed, 2-bath rental run about $8,000. Filter this checklist to Essential to see exactly what to order first.

What is the difference between Essential, Recommended, and Elevated items?

Essential items are the things you cannot host without. Recommended items materially improve guest experience and reviews. Elevated items are the design-forward, five-star touches that help a listing stand out and command higher nightly rates. You can filter the checklist by any tier.

Does this Airbnb furnishing checklist work for any rental style?

Yes. Beyond the core essentials, every decor item offers style-specific product picks for Cabin, Coastal, Desert, and City rentals, so you can furnish to match your property's aesthetic and market.
