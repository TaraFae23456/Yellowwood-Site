The frame

Design is the highest-leverage spend in a short-term rental.

A short-term rental's revenue is set by four numbers: average daily rate (ADR), occupancy, review velocity, and the equity value of the asset itself. Every one of those moves with design.

An undesigned property and a Yellowwood Atelier property collect rent from the same guests, on the same platforms, at very different rates. The math compounds: a higher ADR multiplies across nights, the occupancy lift multiplies across the year, and the photography, reviews, and brand carry the asset's appraised value upward over a 5-year hold.

Investment & ROI

Questions on the numbers.

What's the typical ROI on a Yellowwood Atelier engagement?
Yellowwood Atelier projects target a measurable lift in ADR and occupancy. Recent benchmarks include a 47% ADR lift versus prior pricing and 90% first-month occupancy. Design fees typically pay back inside the first operating year. A property-specific ROI projection is delivered as part of Phase 1 (Strategic Direction).
What is FF&E and how is it billed?
FF&E is the furniture, fixtures, and equipment specified in your design. Yellowwood Atelier bills it at supplier cost plus a transparent 28% procurement fee — no hidden trade markups, no inflated quotes. Residential design firms typically add 45–100% markup on FF&E; Atelier's 28% reflects boutique STR volume pricing and an operator-honest bias. The full FF&E budget is locked at the close of the design phase, and payment is collected 100% before supplier purchase orders are placed.
Are there financing options?
Yellowwood Atelier engagements are billed in milestone draws aligned to project phases — deposit at signing, draws at design close, pre-install, and handoff. The phased structure spreads the investment across the project timeline. Third-party financing options are available case by case for The Yellowwood Portfolio tier.
What's NOT included in the design fee?
FF&E is billed separately at cost plus procurement fee. Travel and lodging for on-site install — future option, custom millwork fabrication, architectural collaboration and permits, general contractor and trades, photography production beyond studio direction, sales tax, freight, and ongoing property management are all estimated outside the design fee. The Investment page above lists the full split.
How does pricing change for The Yellowwood Portfolio tier?
The Yellowwood Portfolio is structured for owners commissioning three or more properties annually. Pricing is bespoke and reflects bulk procurement leverage, shared design language across properties, and ongoing strategic retainer. Portfolio engagements typically deliver lower per-property design fees than single Atelier Engagements due to systemized creative direction and shared sourcing.
What about international or destination projects?
Yellowwood Atelier is U.S.-based and works domestically as a default. International or remote-destination projects are considered case-by-case, with travel, customs, and freight estimated separately and billed at cost.
